🕵️ GRAMMAR

Grammar Detective

Find the sentence that sounds wrong — then fix it out loud.

🔎📱✍️
I usually go to school by bus.
I didn’t went to school yesterday.
She is doing her homework now.
We were tired after the trip.
Fix it aloud: “I didn’t go to school yesterday.”

Which one needs fixing?

She has seen him last week.
She saw him last week.
Good catch. With “last week”, Past Simple is the natural choice.

Which one needs fixing?

If I will have time, I’ll call you.
If I have time, I’ll call you.
Correct correction: “If I have time, I’ll call you.”

📖 READING

Mystery Chat

Read the messages. What is really happening?

📱👀💜
Mia & Chloe
online
Mia: Are we still going to the cinema at 6?
Chloe: I’m really sorry, but I can’t come tonight 😬
Mia: Again? We planned this three days ago.
Chloe: I know. My cousin has just arrived from another city and my parents want me to stay home.
Mia: You could have told me earlier.
Chloe: I only found out 20 minutes ago. Please don’t be angry.
Mia: I’m not angry. Just disappointed.

Quick questions

Chloe has to change the plan because her cousin arrived.
Chloe forgot about the cinema.
Yes — that is the main reason.
Why is Mia disappointed?
Do you think Mia is really “not angry”?
What do you think will happen next?
