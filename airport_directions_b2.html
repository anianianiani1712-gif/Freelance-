Step 1 · Get your bearings

Terminal Map

The corridor isn't just one straight line — it branches off in a few places. Every bend in the map below is a real turn: that's where "turn left" or "turn right" actually happens.

Entrance / Exit Gates Security & Passport Control Toilets 🔴 dot = a real turn (elbow in the corridor)
Direction tip: "left" and "right" depend on which way you're facing — not on the map. The same corner in the corridor can be a left turn or a right turn, depending on your direction of travel. Compare:
Walking TOWARDS the info desk (from the entrance, heading east) → you turn left off the spine.
Walking AWAY from it (from security, heading back west) → the very same corner is now a turn to the right.
Step 2 · Language bank

Phrases You'll Need

B2 level means going beyond "turn left." Use these more natural, fluent expressions.

🗣️ Asking for directions

  • Excuse me, could you tell me how to get to the baggage claim?
  • Sorry to bother you, where's the nearest toilet?
  • I'm trying to find Gate B4 — am I going the right way?
  • Is it far from here, or can I walk?
  • Whereabouts is the duty free shop?

🧭 Giving directions

  • Go straight ahead until you reach...
  • Turn left/right just past the...
  • Take the escalator/lift up to the upper level.
  • It's just past the café, on your right.
  • Bear left/right at the junction, then keep going.
  • Double back / head back the way you came, then turn...

Step 4 · Speak

Pair Roleplay

🎭 Lost at the Airport

Work with a partner. Choose ONE scenario card above that you haven't used yet, and act it out as a real conversation — don't just read the model answer aloud. Add small talk, a "thank you," and at least one follow-up question.

Traveller

  1. Stop a "staff member" politely.
  2. Explain where you are and where you need to go.
  3. Ask at least one follow-up question (e.g. "How long does that take?" / "Is there a lift?").
  4. Thank them and repeat the directions back to check you understood — including any turns.

Airport Staff

  1. Greet the traveller and ask how you can help.
  2. Give clear, step-by-step directions using the language bank.
  3. Be precise about left vs right — check it matches the direction they're walking!
  4. Ask if they have any other questions before they go.
