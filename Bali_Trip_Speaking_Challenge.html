Speaking lesson

My Trip to Bali 🌴

Use your own collage to make your story more vivid, natural and detailed. No long written story first — speak, react, upgrade.

Task 1

🚫 Forbidden Words

Choose one photo from your collage and describe it — but you are NOT allowed to use these words:

beautiful · nice · good · amazing · very
Speak for 30–45 seconds.
Describe the place, food, view or moment. Try to sound precise, not generic.

Useful alternatives

stunningbreathtaking peacefulpicturesque unforgettablevibrant deliciousflavourful crystal-clearcrowded

Quick idea prompts

Possible answers:
“The view was absolutely breathtaking. The sky was glowing orange and pink, and the whole place felt incredibly peaceful.”

“The dish was rich, flavourful and slightly spicy. I’d definitely order it again.”

“The beach looked picturesque, but it was much more crowded than I expected.”
Task 2

👀 Tell Me 3 Things I Can't See

Pick one photo. Tell your teacher three things that are NOT visible in the picture.

Before

What happened just before the photo?

During

What were you thinking / feeling at that moment?

After

What happened next?

Sentence starters:
Before this photo was taken, ...
What you can't see is that ...
At that moment, I was ...
We'd already ... before we ...
A few minutes later, ...

Choose a challenge

“What you can't see is that we'd been waiting for the sunset for almost an hour.”

“While I was taking the photo, the waves were much stronger than they look here.”

“A few minutes later, we found a small café nearby and stayed there until it got dark.”
Task 3

⬆️ Vocabulary Upgrade

Take a basic sentence and make it sound more vivid and natural.

1. The beach was very beautiful.

Basic
The beach was very beautiful.
Better
The beach was absolutely stunning.
Stronger
The beach was one of the most picturesque places I'd ever seen.

2. The food was very good.

Basic
The food was very good.
Better
The food was incredibly tasty.
Stronger
The dish was packed with flavour and completely exceeded my expectations.

3. I was very happy.

Basic
I was very happy.
Better
I was absolutely delighted.
Stronger
I remember feeling genuinely excited and grateful to be there.

Your turn

Say your upgraded version aloud first. You can type it here only if your teacher wants you to save it.

Upgrade toolbox:
very beautiful → stunning / breathtaking / picturesque
very good food → delicious / flavourful / mouth-watering
very happy → delighted / thrilled / over the moon
very calm → peaceful / relaxing / serene
very surprising → unexpected / surprising / mind-blowing
